# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Newgarden staff managing Shopify catalog content and translations across stores and locales. The interface supports extended desktop sessions and remains usable on smaller screens.

## Product Purpose

Find and compare products, prepare translations, review individual fields, and manage stores and preferred terminology. Preserve existing backend behavior and explicit approval before saving translations.

## Brand Personality

Precise, industrial, distinctive. The user requests an equal union of a practical work tool and outstanding professional design, inspired by Nothing.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic industrial decoration, ornamental dashboard widgets, artificial statistics, heavy shadows, and repetitive rounded cards. Do not sacrifice clear controls to the aesthetic.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary task immediately evident on each screen.
- Give operational content and visual craft equal attention.
- Create identity through composition, typography, and restrained details.
- Preserve familiar interactions and existing business workflows.
- Display real state and explain empty or unavailable states honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The user confirmed readable contrast, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion as the baseline. Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, labeled controls, and responsive layouts.
